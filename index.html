LDAC can’t be switched from inside an app. Android picks the Bluetooth codec itself: open Settings › Developer options › Bluetooth audio codec and choose LDAC. On iPhone the EQ starts switched off, because turning it on can make the sound stop when the screen locks (an iOS limitation). If that happens, turn the EQ off and fully restart the app.
